## Exercise library — exercises-dataset

`migrations/0004_exercise_library.sql` are generated by `scripts/build_exercise_dataset.py --library`
from [hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset](https://github.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset), which
redistributes content that originates from ExerciseDB v1. Only the text and data are used.
